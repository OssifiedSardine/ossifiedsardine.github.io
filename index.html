I am a doctoral researcher at the Computational Communication Science Lab at the University of Vienna.

Most of my work is about how people make sense of politics through the media they encounter and the circumstances in which those potential effects are strongest. I have a soft spot for media philosophy and the strange ways new technologies—from prediction markets to language models—change what it feels like to be online. I’m also quite interested in the breakdown and merging of online and offline spaces; the medium, after all, is never neutral.

To try to understand these things, I spend a lot of time building Bayesian models, evaluating language models, and learning about causal inference.

Away from research, I enjoy gambling for chairs and clicking things on the internet. If any of this interests you, or you have something that might interest me (such as chairs), feel free to get in touch. The medium is up to you.
