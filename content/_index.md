I am a doctoral candidate at the [Computational Communication Science Lab](https://compcommlab.univie.ac.at/) at the University of Vienna.

Most of my work is about how people make sense of politics through the media they encounter and the circumstances in which those potential effects are strongest. I have a soft spot for media philosophy and the strange ways new technologies like prediction markets and language models change what it feels like to be online. I'm also quite interested in the breakdown and merging of online and offline spaces. Remember when the internet was a room?

Away from research, I enjoy gambling for chairs and clicking things on my computer. If any of this interests you, or you have something that might interest me (such as chairs), feel free to get in touch. The medium is up to you.
