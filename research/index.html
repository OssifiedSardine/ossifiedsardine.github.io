Working Papers

When, Where, and for Whom Media Exposure Matters: A Comparative Reassessment of Media Effects on Vote Choice 2026

With Jakob-Moritz Eberl, Petro Tolochko & Hajo G. Boomgaarden · Presented at ICA 2026, Cape Town

Uses Bayesian multilevel out-of-sample predictive modelling across 70 elections in 46 countries (CSES) to test when reported political media exposure adds predictive information about vote choice beyond political predispositions.

Too Much Alarmism? Experimental Insights into Misinformation Communication 2025

With Jakob-Moritz Eberl, Marie Heřmanová & Matouš Pilnáček

Preregistered survey experiment in Austria and the Czech Republic testing how alarmist, balanced, and reassuring frames about misinformation shape democratic perceptions, trust, and counter-speech.
